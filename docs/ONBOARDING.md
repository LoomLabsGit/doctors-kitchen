# Onboarding flow (after sign-up)

22 screens, supplied as screenshots by the designer (not captured from the live app; no account, payment or plan was created for this). Mapped on the Miro board in the "Onboarding" frame. IDs match the board: screens are numbered 1 to 22, purple numbers are tap points, orange !1 to !17 are issues (numbered in order of first appearance). Personal details visible in the original screenshots are not reproduced here.

## Flow
1 Sign up > 2 Intro from Dr Rupy > 3 Main goal (pick one) > 4 Follow-up (pick any) > 5 Stat and rating > 6 Photo and quote > 7 7/14/30 day promise > 8 "Do any of these feel familiar?" > 9 Social proof > 10 Food preferences intro > 11 Allergies > 12 Dietary preferences > 13 Foods you dislike > 14 Cooking ability > 15 Summary > 16 Analysing (auto) > 17 Recipes to try (heart or Add all) > 18 Hearts filled > 19 Free trial pitch > 20 Trial reminder (How to cancel opens 21) > 22 Choose your plan.

21 is a modal sheet off 20; 20 also continues to 22.
