# Onboarding flow (after sign-up)

22 screens, supplied as screenshots by the designer (not captured from the live app; no account, payment or plan was created for this). Mapped on the Miro board in the "Onboarding" frame. Personal details visible in the original screenshots are not reproduced here.

## Flow
1 Sign up form > 2 Intro from Dr Rupy > 3 Main goal (pick one) > 4 Follow-up (pick any) > 5 Stat and rating > 6 Photo and quote > 7 7/14/30 day promise > 8 "Do any of these feel familiar?" > 9 Social proof > 10 Food preferences intro > 11 Allergies > 12 Dietary preferences > 13 Foods you dislike > 14 Cooking ability > 15 Summary > 16 Analysing (auto) > 17 Recipes to try (heart or Add all) > 18 Hearts filled > 19 Free trial pitch > 20 Trial reminder (How to cancel opens 21) > 22 Choose your plan.

Screen 21 is a modal sheet off screen 20; screen 20 also continues to 22.

## Findings (O1 to O17)
| ID | Sev | Screens | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| O1 | Med | 5, 6, 7, 9 | Four of the first nine screens are persuasion with no question to answer. |
| O2 | Med | 5, 7 | Health outcome promises with timings and a 91% figure, no source or caveat. |
| O3 | Med | All | No step count or time estimate; thin top bar is the only progress cue. |
| O4 | Low | 3, 4, 6, 9, 15 | Same action worded six ways. |
| O5 | Med | 3, 4 | Goal questions cannot be skipped; small red "Please answer this question". |
| O6 | Med | 8 | Health-sensitive options with no line on why or how used; no "None of these". |
| O7 | Low | 3 to 14 | Selected state is blue, used nowhere else; green on pale chips is low contrast. |
| O8 | Med | 11, 13 | Chips plus typed field; gluten has to be typed; no "None". Matches Recipes U12. |
| O9 | Low | 14 | Only two cooking levels. |
| O10 | Med | 15 | "Allergens: No gluten here" reads as a claim about the food; not editable here; Profile is hidden (U15). |
| O11 | Low | 17, 18 | Add all to favourites gives no confirmation of where hearts were saved. |
| O12 | High | 20, 22 | Prices only on the last screen, after Start for £0. |
| O13 | Med | 19 | Ticks show Premium only, not what is free; no "Not now" visible (to verify). |
| O14 | Low | 20 | Date grey on blue is hard to read; no "no reminder" option. |
| O15 | Low | 21 | Save button with nothing to save; dense terms. |
| O16 | Med | 22 | Per-day price is the headline; billed amounts small; badge clipped; tiny print. |
| O17 | Low | 22 | Restore purchase is iOS-specific (About !2). |
