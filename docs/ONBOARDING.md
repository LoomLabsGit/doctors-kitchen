# Onboarding flow (after sign-up)

22 screens, supplied as screenshots by the designer (not captured from the live app; no account, payment or plan was created for this). Mapped on the Miro board in the "Onboarding" frame. IDs match the board: screens S1 to S22, tap points T1 to T20, issues ON-01 to ON-17 (numbered in order of first appearance). Personal details visible in the original screenshots are not reproduced here.

## Flow
S1 Sign up > S2 Intro from Dr Rupy > S3 Main goal (pick one) > S4 Follow-up (pick any) > S5 Stat and rating > S6 Photo and quote > S7 7/14/30 day promise > S8 "Do any of these feel familiar?" > S9 Social proof > S10 Food preferences intro > S11 Allergies > S12 Dietary preferences > S13 Foods you dislike > S14 Cooking ability > S15 Summary > S16 Analysing (auto) > S17 Recipes to try (heart or Add all) > S18 Hearts filled > S19 Free trial pitch > S20 Trial reminder (How to cancel opens S21) > S22 Choose your plan.

S21 is a modal sheet off S20; S20 also continues to S22.
